# NEURO — Nick's Executive Utility & Reasoning Orchestrator

Personal AI-powered productivity system. Aggregates Jira queue, Obsidian vault, Microsoft 365 (calendar/tasks/email), Strava, location tracking, and AI chat into a single PWA. Runs on Raspberry Pi 5 (16GB) via Tailscale, installed as PWA on iPhone.

## NEURO vs SARA — the division of labour (Nick, 15 Aug 2026)
**NEURO is the brain**, and the NEURO app is Nick's *direct* access to it when he goes looking. **SARA is the J.A.R.V.I.S. layer**: she uses NEURO's brain and **comes to him** — prompts, pushes, helps — and is the visual/audio route for interacting with the brain conversationally rather than by navigating a UI. Neither replaces the other, and **nothing in `sara/` is scaffolding to retire.** Practical consequences: `sara/app` (phone PWA) already talks DIRECTLY to the NEURO backend and never touches `sara/backend` — it is a NEURO client, so there is nothing to "fold in". `sara/backend` (:3005) exists to serve the **Pi 4 desk kiosk** (`sara/frontend`), which is the *ambient* half of SARA. Judge any SARA change by whether it helps her come to Nick; judge any NEURO change by whether it makes the brain better or the direct access clearer.

## ⏳ PARKED DIRECTION — one interface, and it is the chat (Nick, 27 Aug 2026)
Nick's stated longer-term intent: **"maybe the longer term plan should be that there is only one interface — the chat — and we do everything there."** Explicitly parked ("let's pick that up later"), so **do not start migrating screens.** It is recorded because it is the tie-breaker for design decisions taken in the meantime: where two options are otherwise close, prefer the one that makes a surface feel like *talking to SARA* over the one that adds another panel or another tab. It is the same instinct as the Surface refocus below, one level up — and it is corroborated by the measurements taken on 27 Aug (26 flat tabs, `pi-health` the second most-opened screen, `tab_open` falling 266 → 89 → 17 across three weeks). Adding SARA's identity to the standup/EOD conversation was the first step *towards* it and cost nothing if it never happens; retiring a working screen is not, and needs his say-so.

## SARA has no menus (25 Aug 2026) — the refocus
Nick's restatement of the ecosystem: **NEURO collates and directs; the NEURO app is his direct, day-to-day interface to it; SARA is the J.A.R.V.I.S. layer — voice, ears and eyes — and should NOT be a menu.** She surfaces what he needs based on context, rather than waiting to be navigated. Both SARA surfaces are therefore becoming renderers of ONE feed. Phase 1 is built (see the attention pattern below); Phase 2 (phone) and Phase 3 (kiosk) are not.

⚠ **`sara/backend/src/state/inference.js` is the thing to retire.** It had grown an activity enum, a confidence model and a recommended-view map — a second brain — assembled from **seeded** inputs (`state/seed.js`), so it was a second opinion about data that did not exist. Its rules and its honesty are ported into `backend/services/context-state.js` against real inputs; its four-domain contract is NOT ported, because that shape was invented for the seed. `sara/backend` keeps its TRANSPORT half (HA telemetry bus, presence lock, proxy) and loses its decision half.

## SARA stores nothing, and never invents Nick's day (30 Aug 2026)
Three things, all in `sara/backend`, all fixed together because they were one failure wearing three hats: **SARA looked like it was working when it was not.**

⚠ **The kiosk's capture had never worked.** `sara/frontend` has always POSTed to `/api/capture/note` and `/api/capture/todo`, and `sara/backend/server.js` **never mounted a capture route** — every capture made at the desk kiosk 404'd. `src/integrations/neuroCapture.js` + `src/routes/capture.js` are the bridge, and the shape is the point: it **forwards to NEURO's canonical capture routes and stores nothing** (no SARA table, no queue, no local copy — a capture is a NEURO capture or it is not a capture), and **`saved` is true ONLY when NEURO acknowledged it**. Acknowledgement is `success: true`, **not** `res.ok` — a 200 carrying anything else (a proxy error page, a login redirect, a half-written handler) is not an acknowledgement, and treating it as one is precisely how a lost capture gets reported as a saved one. Six named failure reasons (`not-configured` 503 / `unreachable` 504 / `timeout` 504 / `unauthorized` 502 / `rejected` upstream-4xx / `upstream-error` 502), each `saved:false`; an unconfigured bridge **refuses before the network**, because "we were never told where NEURO is" needs a different fix from "NEURO is down". The Capture screen prints `NOT saved — <reason>` and **leaves the words in the box** — clearing it on a failure destroys the one remaining copy of the thought, which is what capture exists to prevent. **Deliberately no offline write queue** (Phase 1 non-goal): a queue that silently holds writes is another way for SARA to become a store. The bridge is a **named door, not an open proxy** — a kind not in the table is refused locally rather than passed through. Nothing logs the captured text or a credential.

⚠ **The State Engine fell back to `seed.js` on every outage.** `stateEngine.js` returned hardcoded content whenever NEURO was unreachable — two invented breaching tickets, an invented probation review, *"Nathan has gone quiet"* against a real colleague, and a specific claim about where Nick physically was ("Office — Little Eaton since 08:40"). A dead feed rendered as a plausible, populated, entirely fictional day, indistinguishable from a working one. `frontend/src/state/presentation.js` held a second copy of the same fiction for when `/api/state` itself failed. Now `src/state/provenance.js` defines four PER-DOMAIN provenances — `neuro` / `neuro-stale` / `unavailable` / `demo` — carried per domain AND rolled up into `model.provenance` — ⚠ **the ROLL-UP has a FIFTH value, `mixed`** ("partly live"), which is what the live kiosk is usually in; a consumer switching on provenance with only four cases falls through and renders a partly-good read as total blindness (caught on the Pi panel, 30 Aug 2026), with `frontend/src/components/ConnectionStatus.jsx` as the banner (**silent when live**, or a permanent green badge is one nobody reads by week two). Two rules do the work: an unread domain is **structurally empty and null, never 0** (`open: null`, so no screen can read a fact out of it), and the briefing **never says "Queue is calm" over a queue it could not see** — it says it cannot see it and that this is not an all-clear. `seed.js` survives for **demo only**: reachable solely under `SARA_DEMO_MODE=true`, stamped `demo` end to end, and **refused outright under `NODE_ENV=production`**. Pinned by a negative test asserting no seeded name can appear in production state. Also: a failed poll now **degrades to `stale` keeping the last good payload** (with `ageMs`) rather than blanking, for up to `MAX_STALE_MS` (15 min) — but **losing the configuration is not an outage** and drops straight to `not-configured`, because data whose origin is no longer configured has no provenance left. A **stale connection outranks the domain roll-up** for `provenance.state`, or "NEURO stopped answering four minutes ago" hides behind "partly live".

⚠ **The NEURO dependency was implicit.** `neuroSnapshot.js` and `neuroChat.js` each carried `https://nuero.nickward.co.uk` as a **default base URL**, so an unconfigured SARA silently reached a public host over the open internet *and reported itself available*. There is **no default any more**: `src/integrations/neuroConfig.js` is the one place that knows where NEURO is and how it authenticates (`NEURO_API_TOKEN` preferred over `NEURO_PIN` — SARA is a machine client), it is **validated at startup** and exposed non-sensitively on `GET /api/health` under `neuro` (`configured` / `ready` / `baseUrl` / `credentialConfigured` / `credentialKind` / `problems`), and it reports **whether** a credential is set, never what it is. It **never refuses to boot** on a bad config — the kiosk is also where a PIN gets entered, so a SARA that will not start cannot be repaired from the device in front of you. ⚠ Two consequences worth knowing: **a deployed `.env` without `NEURO_BASE_URL` now reports not-configured instead of silently working**, and NEURO's **`/api/queue/summary` no longer exists** (the Jira queue was deleted in July 2026), so it is marked `optional` in the snapshot — a permanently-404ing endpoint counted against reach would pin the snapshot at "partial" for ever and make the word meaningless. Pinned by `capture.test.js` (17, incl. real HTTP wiring — the original bug WAS a routing bug), `neuroSnapshot.test.js` (7) and the rewritten `stateEngine.test.js`.

## NEURO vs VANTAGE — the division of labour (3 Sep 2026)
**NEURO is INBOUND and PERSONAL. VANTAGE is DEPARTMENTAL and DIRECTIVE.** NEURO collects what arrives at Nick — meetings, email, tickets, the vault, his body, his diary — and answers *what do I owe, and what should I do next*. VANTAGE looks at the SERVICE DESK and answers *what is going wrong, and what should we do about it*; it is also his coaching layer. Both deal in tasks, which is exactly why the boundary needs writing down: it is a **more** confusable boundary than NEURO-vs-SARA, and the two repos are `Wardy-uk/nuero` and `Wardy-uk/vantage` (working directory `Service Desk Continual Improvement` — three names for one thing).

⚠ **The department services in this repo are HOSTED here, not OWNED here.** `weekly-risk`, `management-log`, `overtime`, `team-health`, `development-plan`, `evidence-register`, `knowledge-gaps` and `nova-121-*` are all departmental by subject; they live in NEURO because NEURO existed first. That is a fact about history, not a decision about ownership, and it is the thing most likely to be mistaken for one. **A new departmental feature goes to VANTAGE unless there is a reason written down.**

**How they talk.** VANTAGE → NEURO over HTTP, and it is a CLOSED SET of **five writes** (`neuro.js`'s header is the register of them, and its count is load-bearing): create a task, queue a suggestion, score a dedupe match, link a task to Microsoft, put a line on the weekly report. Everything else it does is a GET. NEURO → VANTAGE is one direct SQLite read (below). Nothing else connects them.

**The criticality handoff (3 Sep 2026).** `vantage/backend/services/criticality.js` is the single place that decides whether a VANTAGE finding becomes a NEURO **task** (`direct`) or a **pending action** in the approval queue (`pending`). It errs towards `pending`: a wrong `direct` puts unasked-for work in the list Nick uses to decide what to do next, which is the failure that got auto-promote removed. NEURO's half is `POST /api/actions` (intake), `criticality` on `POST /api/tasks` (**provenance only — NEURO stores the claim and never re-derives it**), the `vantage_suggestion` presenter case and its executor. `VANTAGE_AUTO_PUSH_ENABLED` gates VANTAGE's own timer, which writes unattended and only ever on the `direct` route.

⚠ **`services/estate-cost.js` reads VANTAGE's SQLite file DIRECTLY**, on the Pi, outside the HTTP contract above — the only place either system reaches into the other's storage. It depends on VANTAGE's `docs` table (`docs.collection`, `docs.json`), which is a JSON-document store rather than a modelled schema, and it reads the `llm_calls` collection for the AI spend panel. That shape is VANTAGE's to change, so **changing it is a decision that breaks NEURO's estate panel silently** — the read degrades to "no data", which looks exactly like a quiet week. The live database is **`/mnt/data/vantage-data/vantage.db`** — which is `estate-cost.js`'s compiled-in default and needs no `VANTAGE_DB_PATH` on the Pi. ⚠ There is a **decoy**: `/mnt/data/vantage/backend/data/vantage.db` is a stale 16KB file created by VANTAGE's own `db.js` default (`__dirname/data`), and querying it returns an empty `docs` table — which reads as "VANTAGE has no findings" rather than as "wrong file". Checked 3 Sep 2026, after being caught by exactly that.

**Vocabulary, because four things are called some variant of "action".** VANTAGE has **findings** (something spotted, with a severity and a tense) and **plan actions** (items from the Support Review improvement plan). NEURO has **tasks** (the `tasks` table, the source of truth), **action items** (`action-items.js` — checkboxes in vault notes), **action candidates** (`action-candidates.js` — commitments extracted from meeting notes, pending review as `capture_todo`) and **actions** (`sara_actions`, the approval queue). A finding is not a task until something routes it into one.

## Tech Stack
- **Backend:** Express 4 + Node.js (CommonJS, `require()` — NOT ESM)
- **Frontend:** React 19 + CSS modules (per-component `.css` files) + Vite
- **Database:** better-sqlite3 (native SQLite, WAL). Writes commit immediately — no flush step, and external scripts can read while the server runs.
- **Auth:** PIN-based (`X-NEURO-PIN` header) + API token for machine clients (n8n). ⚠ **The PIN was ROTATED on 17 Aug 2026 (#123)** — the old value had been committed in plaintext to `docs/sara-watch-siri-mvp.md` since 15 July, and the repo is PUBLIC. Never write the PIN into a tracked file, a doc, a test fixture or a commit message; read it from `backend/.env`. Rotating it breaks every client that sends it (Watch/Siri shortcut, both PWAs, the MCP server's `NEURO_PIN`, any n8n flow not using the API token), so a rotation is not done until those are updated.
- **AI:** Anthropic SDK (Claude) + Ollama (local models on Pi) with AI routing layer
- **External:** Jira REST, Microsoft Graph (MSAL device code), Strava OAuth, OwnTracks, Obsidian vault filesystem, n8n webhooks, web-push notifications
- **Monorepo:** npm workspaces — `backend/`, `frontend/`, `worker/`
- **Worker:** RETIRED 2026-08-15 (`PI4_WORKER_ENABLED=false`). The Pi 4 managed 12 tokens/sec prompt processing on the SMALLEST model available, so a ~3,700-token triage payload needed five minutes just to read the prompt. Not a model-sizing problem — a hardware ceiling. Code and config are left in place so re-enabling is one env var. Background tasks now route: heavy prose work (email_triage, transcript_processing) to cloud, light work (import_classification, journal_prompts) to Pi 5 Ollama.

### SARA mobile (`sara/app/`)
- The PWA on Nick's phone. **The root view is `views/Surface.jsx` (25 Aug 2026) and the tab strip is no longer navigation** — it renders `GET /api/attention`: one thing, in the context the brain decided it in. The other nine views are still all here, but as places the brain routes TO. ⚠ **The strip is HIDDEN, not deleted** — `navOpen` reveals it via "Show me everything", and it stays revealed while Nick is off the Surface, because the one screen with no menu must not also be the only way back. Nick's failure mode is avoidance: a thing he cannot find is worse than a menu he does not need, and an ambient surface that is sometimes wrong must always have a way round it. `.app__nav[hidden]{display:none}` is load-bearing — the rule above it is `display:flex`, which beats the bare `hidden` attribute, so without it the escape hatch renders permanently open. Three renderings the Surface must keep distinct, because conflating them is how a broken feed comes to look like a good day: **pool unavailable** ("I can't see your work — this isn't an all-clear"), **quiet** (in a meeting / day off), and **genuinely nothing pending**. It renders `context.unknowns`, `gaps` and `dropped` rather than swallowing them — held is not lost, and "couldn't look" is not "nothing there". Speech uses the brain's pre-composed `speech` line and its `quiet` flag; the phrasing is NOT rebuilt client-side, since three surfaces saying the same thing differently is how they drift. Card→tab routing reuses `resolveSaraLiteTab`, so a card and the notification for the same thing cannot land on different tabs.
- Seven tabs became nine (#26), then ten with the Surface.
- `views/Tasks.jsx` is the browsable task list — before it, capture was one-way on mobile and the only way to tick something off was to arrive via a notification. Reads `/api/todos/focus` so its ranking matches Focus and the rest of NEURO.
- `completeTask.js` is the ONE place that knows how to close a task, shared with `NotificationActionCard`. Owner order is `task_id` → `ms_id` → `filePath`+`lineNumber`; getting it wrong is silent (a null `filePath` just 400s and the task stays open).
- Tab ids must also exist in `SARA_LITE_TABS` in `shared/action-surfaces.cjs`, or notification routing falls back to Focus.
- **Voice loop (15 Aug 2026, #90)** — `sara/app/src/voiceUtils.js` started as a verbatim copy of `frontend/src/voiceUtils.js` and has since **diverged, deliberately**: the shared version was only ever proven on desktop, and three things it does are wrong on iOS. (1) The gesture unlock spoke an EMPTY utterance — iOS drops a zero-length one without queueing it, so the unlock silently no-ops and every later `speak()` is ignored; it now speaks a single space at volume 0, and `unlockAudio` is exported so the 🔊 tap can unlock explicitly rather than relying on whichever touch wins the `{once:true}` race. (2) `cancel()` ran unconditionally before every `speak()`, a known way to wedge iOS; it is now guarded on `speaking||pending` and followed by `resume()`. (3) `speakSara` returns the utterance so callers can watch `onstart`/`onerror`. **Port these back to `frontend/` before touching either copy** — they are harmless on desktop and the two files are meant to track each other. `pickVoice()` falls through an en-GB ladder, logging the full voice list when nothing matches. `views/Chat.jsx` owns both halves: a persisted 🔊/🔇 toggle (`sara_voice_out`, OFF by default — a PWA that talks unprompted on a train gets disabled forever) and a 🎤 composer button. **Speech-out is gated on `busy`, never on `messages`** — keying that effect on `messages` speaks every streamed token. Dictation is `continuous`, so the stop tap is what sends (also the iOS audio-unlock gesture); `onend` fires from a stale closure, hence `dictatedRef`/`busyRef` rather than state. Note the `voice` tab mounts **Capture** with `autoRecord`, not Chat — the talk→hear loop lives on the Chat tab. Chat only: briefings and nudges (NEURO's `speakIfEnabled`) are not spoken on the phone yet, and there is no wake word (#11 parked).

### VESTA (`vesta/`)
- The shared home surface on **vesta.nickward.co.uk** (Netlify, base dir `vesta`, push to main), talking to NEURO's one public mount `/api/v`. React + Vite, port 5176 in dev. SARA is Nick's; VESTA is the household's.
- ⚠ **`VITE_API_URL` is the Pi's public Tailscale FUNNEL address, NOT the `.ts.net` tailnet one `sara/app` uses.** Her phone is outside the house and has no Tailscale, which is the entire reason `/api/v` is exempt from the PIN.
- ⚠ **The client enforces NOTHING and must never start.** Redaction is `services/vesta.js` (a work event's subject and location are ABSENT from the response object, not hidden by CSS); scopes are re-checked on every route; a private catalogue answers the same 404 a missing one does. `scopes` from `login` decides what to **render** — a convenience, never the boundary. Filtering a title in a component means something upstream has broken.
- **Three renderings that must stay distinct**, because conflating them is how the surface starts lying: **gap** (the server could not read it — amber, names why, *"don't treat it as nothing"*), **empty** (read fine, nothing there), and **absent** (no permission — the section is not mounted at all). *"I couldn't read the kitchen"* and *"the fridge is empty"* send her to different shops. Same rule on `meals.known === false`, which means *nothing is recorded*, not *there is nothing to eat*. `Section.jsx` is the one place that knows the difference.
- ⚠ **Calendar times are SLICED OUT OF THE STRING, never parsed into a `Date`.** The backend already asked Graph for Europe/London wall-clock times; re-parsing re-applies an offset and shows every BST event an hour out — the bug NEURO's calendar had once already.
- The kitchen renders off **`kitchenSections`** (display names) and keys into `items` by the lowercased name. Rendering off `items` alone loses his capitalisation and silently drops a section he has created but not yet filled — an empty Freezer must still show, or she cannot add to it.
- Until Nick creates her account (with scopes) AND a `kitchen` catalogue marked `shared: true`, the app is correct and empty — it **says which is missing** rather than rendering a blank panel that looks broken. Full detail in `vesta/README.md`; the build brief is `docs/handoff-vesta.md`.
- ⚠ **The tasks path was DEAD on arrival and nothing noticed (31 Aug 2026).** `routes/vesta.js` passed `req.account.username` to `capture.submissions()` and `capture.submit()`, which take the **account OBJECT** — so `sourceFor()` keyed on `capture:undefined`, every read returned `[]` and every write 429'd *"no such account"*. **VESTA's first feature had never worked**, behind a green suite, because the only assertion was `assert.ok(Array.isArray(json.tasks))` — true of `[]`, which is precisely what the broken path returns. Identical species to the `upsertCalendarEvent` camelCase trap the same day (guessing snake_case from the schema writes a row of nulls, and *every* "the subject didn't leak" assertion passes on it, because an empty calendar contains no customer names either). **The rule both cost: PAIR EVERY NEGATIVE ASSERTION WITH A POSITIVE ONE.** Pinned now by a round trip (add a task → it comes back on her home screen, with its four-key shape) plus a negative that one account never sees another's.

### VESTA accounts, in Settings (`components/VestaAccounts.jsx`)
- The admin half of VESTA, in the NEURO sidebar under **Settings**, against `/api/capture-links` behind the PIN. Create an account, change what it sees, reset a PIN (which also clears a lockout), revoke. Before it, adding someone was a curl command against the Pi.
- ⚠ **`setScopes` had shipped with NO ROUTE** — reachable from the test suite and nowhere else — so the only way to widen an account was to DELETE it and issue a new PIN, over a change that is none of that person's business. `POST /api/capture-links/:username/scopes` closes it. **Omitting the `scopes` field is a 400, and is deliberately NOT the same as sending `[]`** (which legitimately narrows back to tasks-only): treating a missing field as the defaults would silently change an account nobody asked to change.
- The checkbox vocabulary comes from **`GET /api/capture-links/scopes`**, never a copy in the component — a second list is how a screen offers a permission that does not exist. ⚠ That literal path sits on a router that also has `/:username/pin` and `/:username/scopes`; Express matches in registration order and this codebase has shipped a literal path swallowed as a parameter before, so it is **registered first** and pinned by a test asserting it returns the vocabulary rather than an account named "scopes".
- `tasks` is not togglable — an account without it is a login that can do nothing, which is a confusing way to spell "disabled". Enforced by `normaliseScopes`, with the checkbox merely disabled so it does not look like a choice.
- Verified live end to end: a scope change takes effect on the **EXISTING token** with no re-login (`requireAccount` re-reads the account every request, which is what makes revocation immediate), and revoking kills a live session on its next call. Pinned by `capture-links-scopes.test.js` (7, real HTTP).

### VESTA photo → items (`services/vesta-vision.js`)
- ⚠ **Switched from NEURO → Settings → Switches** (`feature-flags` key `vesta_photo`), not an SSH session and a pm2 restart. `isEnabled()` asks the registry rather than reading `process.env` directly, so `VESTA_PHOTO_ENABLED` still WINS when it is set — the panel then disables the control and says which variable owns it, instead of offering a toggle that silently does nothing. Unset (as on the Pi) the stored value decides. **Defaults FALSE**, and that IS Nick's gate from the handoff — *"do this last, after the typed path is proven; if the list is wrong, a photo just makes it wrong faster."* Same idiom as `DAY_PLANNER_ENABLED` / `NOTION_SYNC_ENABLED`. Lifting it is his call.
- **It PROPOSES and never writes.** `POST /api/v/catalogue/:slug/scan` returns a list she ticks; each kept item then goes through the SAME `/add` route a typed one does. Pinned by a test asserting the **vault file** is byte-identical after a scan — a route that answered politely while writing would pass any check made on its own output.
- ⚠ **The photo is never persisted, logged, echoed back or stored.** It lives in memory for one request. A photo of a kitchen is a photo of a home — the post on the worktop, a prescription, a laptop screen, whoever is standing in it. Stricter than the rest of VESTA needs, because this is the only route that receives what a camera saw.
- ⚠ **It is the only route on the PUBLIC mount that spends money**, so it is gated three deep: the `kitchen` scope, the flag, and a **per-account rolling-24h cap** (`withinCap`, pure — a window, never a total that resets on restart, which is the bug `ai-routing`'s counter had). The slot is claimed **before** the call, so an expensive failure still costs one; the read-modify-write is **synchronous with no `await` between** (`plaud-admin-blocks.acquireLock`'s rule — a real mutex in one process, not across them).
- ⚠ **`/api/v` gets its own 6mb body parser REGISTERED BEFORE the global 50mb one** in `server.js`. body-parser skips a request whose body is already parsed, so **the order is the whole mechanism** — swapping those two lines silently republishes the 50mb ceiling to the internet. Verified live: an 8MB post answers 413.
- **An unreadable answer REFUSES; it never returns an empty list** — "I couldn't read the photo" and "there's no food in the photo" are different facts, and an empty array would render as a fridge she has just been told is bare. An empty array from the model IS a real answer (an empty worktop exists) and is told apart by `ok`. A truncated array (the too-small-`max_tokens` shape that cost email triage whole runs) refuses rather than keeping the part that parsed.
- **An invented section becomes `null`, never itself** — `catalogue.addItem` refuses a section it does not know, so passing "Larder" through would fail at the moment she taps Confirm, with the error attached to the item rather than the guess. Null renders as a real choice she makes, and is deliberately NOT defaulted to the first shelf.
- ⚠ **A refusal is HTTP 200 with `stop_reason: "refusal"`, not a thrown error** — reading `.content` without checking turns a decline into an empty answer. Handled in `anthropic-provider.vision()`, which is where every Anthropic SDK call lives. The API error message is **never** passed through to her (it can name a model, a key state or an account, and this mount is public) — logged for Nick, generalised for her.

## Key Patterns
- **CommonJS throughout backend** — `require()` / `module.exports`. NOT ESM. No `import` statements.
- **PIN auth middleware** — all `/api/*` routes require `X-NEURO-PIN` header or `?pin=` query param. Machine clients use `X-NEURO-API-TOKEN` instead.
- **State KV store** — `db.getState(key)` / `db.setState(key, value)` for runtime state (sync status, last errors, etc.)
- **AI routing (policy rewritten 14 Aug 2026)** — `ai-routing.js` routes by **latency need, not by vendor ladder**. `LATENCY_SENSITIVE_TASKS` (chat, standup/EOD interactive + questions, email draft/summary, briefing synthesis) → **OpenRouter first**, because the Pi's local models are too slow to hold a conversation with. Everything else — light, scheduled, background — → **Ollama first**, cloud only if local fails. `_providerOrder(taskType)` is the single source of that decision and both `runTask` and `runStreamingChat` iterate it. **Anthropic and OpenAI are backstops, never the default**: they were tiers 1 and 2 until the Anthropic key ran out of credit and, being first in line, took chat and the standup down with it. Pinned by `ai-routing.test.js` — if you change the order, those tests should fail. Tool calling: `getToolProvider(taskType)` picks the provider for a tools turn (OpenRouter preferred, Anthropic fallback, null if neither) — both providers expose an identical `chatWithTools(systemPrompt, messages, tools, runTool, options)` returning `{text, usage, toolCalls}`, so callers stay provider-agnostic. Ollama has no function-calling API, so no cloud provider = no tools, and chat/standup degrade to plain conversation rather than pretending.
- **One vault exclude list (15 Aug 2026, #85/#34/#86)** — `services/vault-exclusions.js` is the single source of what is not content. It separates **generated** (`Vault Audit`, `SARA Import Reports`, and by FILENAME the task export / `Master Todo` / MoSCoW worksheets / `.backup-` / `.sync-conflict-`) from **retired** (`Archive`, `_toDelete`), because a linter resolving link *targets* still needs to see the retired half — that distinction is the fix for #81, not an oversight. `Daily/` is excluded from embeddings and **kept** for entity extraction: "who did I mention on Tuesday" is exactly what a daily note answers, and exactly what a semantic index of hundreds of scratchpads ruins. Used by `embeddings.js` and `entities.js`; the other copies (`obsidian.js`, `people-gap.js`, `plaud-sync.js`, `one-to-one-detect.js`) are deliberately untouched for now — each is scoped to its own job. **Skipping on the walk only stops NEW rows**, so both consumers prune: `rebuildEmbeddings` drops rows no longer indexable, `entities.pruneExcludedEntities()` runs at the head of the nightly sweep.
- **Embeddings are multi-chunk (15 Aug 2026, #79)** — `prepareFile` returns EVERY chunk; it used to compute them all and return `chunks[0]`, so the DB held 3,216 rows all at `chunk_index 0` and everything decided in the back half of a meeting was unreachable by search, chat RAG and the MCP tools. Invisible, because search always returns something. Bounded at `MAX_CHUNKS_PER_FILE = 60`, logged when hit — **sized against the live vault, not guessed**: 1,091 indexable notes, median 4 chunks, p90 20, p99 60, max 151. The first cut used 20 and would have truncated 107 files, i.e. the top 10% and precisely the long transcripts this fix exists for. The bound is for cost on outliers only; result dominance is handled by the best-chunk-per-file fold, not by this number. A full re-index is **~8,400 chunks** against 3,215 before. Three invariants: the unchanged-check compares content hash **AND** `getEmbeddingChunkCount` (or files indexed under the old code look done forever); rows are **deleted before re-save** (a shortened note has fewer chunks and `INSERT OR REPLACE` alone orphans the tail); and `semanticSearch` folds to the **best chunk per file** before slicing, or `maxResults` counts chunks and one transcript fills the results with itself. `isConfigured()` reads `VOYAGE_API_KEY` — it read `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`, a different vendor, and only worked because both are set on the Pi. Pinned by `embeddings.test.js`. **The index does not improve until a rebuild runs.**
- **Graph collections are paged (15 Aug 2026, #29)** — `graphFetchAll()` follows `@odata.nextLink`; `$top` is a page size, never the answer. Used by `calendarView`, To Do tasks and Planner. Two traps: `graphFetch` returns `null` on 401 and callers read that as "use the NOVA bridge", so the helper returns `null` rather than an empty collection — an empty one reads as *"you have no meetings"* and the fallback never fires. And a page cap or a mid-collection failure sets `truncated: true` **and logs**, because a silent cap is the bug (third instance of that species in two days: calendar `$top=50`, the 1,958-key JQL, Planner `$top=200`).

- **A transcript is pulled once, so it is pulled when the speakers have names (1 Sep 2026)** — the
speaker-naming gate in `plaud-sync.js` (`assessSpeakerNaming` + `decideSpeakerHold`, both PURE) plus
`pendingSpeakers` in the sync ledger, and the Plaud cron moved **30 → 15 minutes**. PLAUD assigns real
names some minutes AFTER the transcript itself lands, and nothing re-pulls a recording once it is
ledgered — so pulling the moment a transcript exists reliably captured it at its least useful:
*"Speaker 2"* throughout, permanently. **The signal was already in data the sync had already paid for**
— every segment carries BOTH `speaker` and `original_speaker`, so an unnamed voice is one whose
`speaker` still equals its raw label. Verified live: a named 1-2-1 reads `"Nick Ward" / "Speaker 1"`,
an unnamed one `"Speaker 2" / "Speaker 2"`. **Deterministic — no model call, no cost**, and the check
itself costs no API calls because [the transcript is fetched before the readiness decision anyway]
(`fetchTranscriptSegments`, split out of `fetchTranscriptBody` for exactly this).

  ⚠ **THE DEADLINE IS WHAT MAKES HOLDING SAFE, so every path that cannot measure it PULLS** — an
unreadable or missing first-seen stamp, an unreadable `now`, no transcript to judge on. A hold that can
outlive its own clock is a recording that never arrives, and a transcript nobody can find is far worse
than an unattributed one. On expiry it pulls ANYWAY and **stamps the note** (`speakers_named`, plus a
line in the body), because an unattributed transcript that looks identical to an attributed one is how
a "Speaker 2" quote ends up in somebody's mouth. ⚠ **`speakers_named` is THREE-VALUED** — `null` is
"never judged" (no transcript, or a note predating this), which is not the fact "judged, and never
named". ⚠ **The clock is PERSISTED per recording, never an in-memory timer**: the backend restarts
several times a day on deploys, so an in-process clock would reset the hour on each one and the hold
could outlive every deadline. Stamped per recording, never batched to the end of a run.

  ⚠ **A SOLO recording is never held** — one voice has nothing to disambiguate, so PLAUD never names it
and waiting burns the full hour on every memo to pull exactly what was there at the start. ⚠ **A slot
counts as named if ANY of its segments names it**: attribution is per utterance and PLAUD is not
consistent across a long meeting, so requiring every line to agree would hold a fully named recording
on one stray label for ever. ⚠ **It says names were ASSIGNED, never that they are CORRECT** — live data
has a segment labelled "Nick Ward" whose raw label is "Speaker 2", two voices merged; nothing here can
see that and nothing here claims to. Held recordings are **not ledgered**, so `shouldProcessRecording`
re-offers them, and the 14-day lookback keeps them in the list window. Surfaced on `getStatus().speakerWait`
— a recording NEURO has deliberately not written must be visible, or it is silently absent from the vault
while everything reports healthy. The cost is a re-fetch per held recording per cycle (~4 instead of 1
over the hour); a tick with nothing pending costs ONE `list_files`. `PLAUD_SPEAKER_WAIT_ENABLED=false`
switches it off, `PLAUD_SPEAKER_WAIT_MINUTES` moves the deadline. ⚠ **Repull NEVER waits** — it is the
manual recovery for a MISSING recording, and holding the one route back for an hour is the wrong trade;
it pulls and stamps what it found. Pinned by `plaud-speaker-wait.test.js` (18).

- **The index walked to depth 4 while search walked to 12 (31 Aug 2026)** — `services/vault-walk.js` is now
the ONE traversal, shared by `retrieval.js` and `embeddings.listVaultFiles()`. They had disagreed about how
much vault exists: retrieval was fixed to depth 12 with a reported cap and the embeddings inventory was
still hard-coded at **depth 4**, so a note in `Meetings/2026/08/deep/` was findable by keyword and **could
never be indexed for semantic search** — permanently, silently, and invisibly, because a hybrid search
always returns something. The DEPTH/CAP policy is shared; the EXCLUSION policy stays per-caller on purpose
(`Daily/` is out of embeddings and deliberately kept for entities; retrieval additionally drops `Templates`
and `Vault Audit`). ⚠ **An unreadable folder is now RECORDED, not skipped** — both old walkers did
`try{readdir}catch{continue}`, so a broken permission or an unmounted disk produced a smaller answer that
called itself complete.
  ⚠ **`semanticAvailable` only ever meant "the query embedded".** It said nothing about whether the index
holds the vault, so a half-built index answered every search looking whole. `embeddings.refreshCoverage()`
measures **eligible / indexed / unindexed / stale / failed / truncated / excluded / inaccessible** and
stores it in `agent_state.embeddings_coverage`; `getCoverage()` is a **cheap synchronous KV read** on the
search path and the walk happens on a schedule (25s after boot, hourly at :35, and at the end of every
rebuild) — the `team-availability` live→cache split, for the same reason. `db.getEmbeddingIndexSummary()`
exists because `getAllEmbeddings()` loads every 1024-float vector, and reading the whole index to count it
is how a health check becomes the reason the Pi is slow. **A failed run records WHICH FILES** in
`agent_state.embeddings_failed` — their old rows deliberately survive, so from the outside a partly failed
rebuild is otherwise identical to a clean one. ⚠ **`complete` is THREE-VALUED**: `true` / `false` /
**`null` = never measured**, which is reported as its own state and never as `true`; it is durable, so
`null` is only ever seen on a brand-new install. ⚠ **A scope narrows what COUNTS as a gap** —
`coverageForScope` — but a partial WALK is never scoped away, and the scope is never widened to compensate.
  `health` gains `semanticCoverageComplete` / `semanticCoverageReasons` / `semanticIndexedCount` /
`semanticEligibleCount` / `semanticUnindexedPaths` (bounded sample; the counts are exact), and
`describeIncompleteness()` treats a coverage gap **exactly like** a dead provider or a capped walk, because
all three produce the identical short list. `health.truncated` deliberately keeps its narrower meaning
(traversal only) so existing consumers do not shift under them.
- **A sibling directory shares the prefix (31 Aug 2026)** — `routes/vault.js` containment. The guard was
`resolved.startsWith(path.resolve(VAULT_PATH))`, a **STRING** test: with the vault at `C:\Vault`, the path
`../Vault-old/secret.md` resolves to `C:\Vault-old\secret.md`, which starts with `C:\Vault` — so it
**passed**, and read / write / append / list / **delete** / export all operated outside the vault on a path
the guard had just approved. Not theoretical: `nuero-vault` has siblings on the Pi, and the vault holds a
`Personal/` folder of disciplinary prep, a fraud investigation and OH documents. Now `path.relative` decides
(`..`, `..<sep>`, or absolute = outside), plus: a **missing or unreadable vault root REFUSES** rather than
resolving against the process working directory (the capture drop-box bug, one service along), a NUL byte
is refused, and **symlink escape** is closed by `realpath` on the deepest EXISTING ancestor — which is what
lets a genuinely new file still be created while stopping one being created *through* an escaping link. The
two `path.join(safeDir, filename)` sites (export-docx, person-doc) go back through the guard rather than
being trusted because their halves looked fine apart. Pinned by `vault-path-safety.test.js` (21, real HTTP;
the symlink case skips honestly where Windows will not create one).
- **An invalid date became an UNBOUNDED search (31 Aug 2026)** — `retrieval.parseDateRange` /
`parseDateBound`, both PURE. `new Date('lastweek')` is `Invalid Date` and **every comparison against NaN is
false**, so the old filter excluded nothing: a typo'd bound silently searched all of time and returned the
answer labelled as a date range. Now only a real `YYYY-MM-DD` or a full ISO timestamp is accepted;
`2026-02-31` is refused rather than rolled into March; an inverted range is refused; and the route answers
**400 with the offending field**. ⚠ **Omission is not a typo** — an absent bound keeps the 30-day default,
an invalid one is an error, and conflating them is what made this invisible. ⚠ **A date-only `to` now means
the END of that day** — `to=2026-08-31` used to mean midnight and dropped the whole of the 31st from the one
search whose entire purpose is a date range. The response returns **normalised ISO bounds** plus
`range.fromDefaulted`/`toDefaulted`. The range check runs **BEFORE** the vault check, because a caller error
is the same error whatever the environment. Pinned by `temporal-range.test.js` (14) + 7 route tests.
- **Brain Health moved out of SARA into NEURO (31 Aug 2026)** — `components/BrainHealthPanel.jsx`, sidebar
**Brain Health** (view id `brain-health`), replacing SARA's `brain` tab (`BrainManagement`, deleted). The
division of labour decides it: NEURO is the brain and the NEURO app is Nick's DIRECT access to it, while
SARA comes to him — and vault maintenance is neither ambient nor something worth interrupting him about.
It is a desk job with dated reports to read and writes to weigh, and on a phone the reports were unreadable.
⚠ **Removing a SARA tab is never only a deletion**: an id missing from `SARA_LITE_TABS` routes its
notifications to Focus IN SILENCE. The notification KIND `brain` is deliberately KEPT (`vault_hygiene`,
`plaud`, `imports`, `knowledge_reflection`, `sweep_complete` still resolve to it, and the desktop still
navigates to Imports); only its SARA destination is gone, so it now falls through to the **Surface** like
everything else with no dedicated tab. Two things the SARA screen got wrong and this fixes: **read and
WRITE were the same grey Run button** — `Plan links` previews while `Apply links` and `Connect orphans`
edit real notes and `PLAUD repull` re-downloads recordings — so effect is now a per-job badge AND the card's
left edge (unmarked / amber / blue), and **nothing said what an action would do**, so every job states what
it READS, what it CHANGES, what it will NOT do, how to get back (`Scripts/.lint-backups/` + changelog) and
where the report lands. Anything that writes is **two-step**: it refuses until its own preview has run, and
the confirm quotes that preview's real numbers — the `event-parser` / `one-to-one-booking` / `task-blocks`
idiom. A failed scan renders *"missing, not zero"* rather than a clean bill of health. Pinned by
`brain-health-migration.test.js` (9, source scans with positive controls), incl. that the vault-hygiene
notification still resolves to a tab that exists.

## Deployment
- Runs on Raspberry Pi 5 (16GB) at home
- Accessible via Tailscale VPN
- Frontend built with Vite, served by Express in production
- PWA installed on iPhone
- Worker runs on separate Pi 4
- **Node 22.22.2 must be on PATH for any pm2 command** — Node 20 segfaults better-sqlite3.
- ⚠ **Never pipe a deploy step to `head`/`tail`.** A pipeline's exit status is the LAST command, so a failed `git pull` sails straight through an `&&` chain and the Pi builds, tests and restarts at the PREVIOUS commit — it cost a silent wrong-commit deploy on 16 Aug. Confirm with `git log --oneline -1` **on the target**, never with the pull's own output. Equally: **never scp a scratch script into the Pi's repo working tree** (use `/tmp`), because an untracked file there blocks the next `--ff-only` pull.
- ⚠ **Before exempting any route from auth, run `tailscale serve status` first.** pi5 serves `https://pi5.tailecb90f.ts.net` → `127.0.0.1:3001` with **Funnel ON**, and Tailscale proxies both tailnet AND public traffic from loopback — so a guard trusting `127.0.0.1` publishes the route to the internet. Identity must come from `Tailscale-User-Login` / `Tailscale-Funnel-Request`, failing CLOSED on bare loopback.

### Backups (#59, 16 Aug 2026)
Local: `backup-data.sh` keeps **28** rotated DB copies under `/mnt/data/backups/nuero-db`. ⚠ The DB nearly doubled with the Apple Health backfill (235 → 447 MB), so that directory is heading for **~12.5 GB** as the rotation cycles — survivable (422 GB free), but it is a decision, not a surprise.

Off-site: nightly root cron 02:20, rclone `crypt` to Backblaze B2 bucket `pi5-neuro-offsite`, filenames AND directory names encrypted, watchdog-monitored. **Ships only the half that cannot be regenerated** — vault + Home Assistant + ONE current `agent.db` + syncthing config; `backups/` is excluded deliberately (rotated copies of the same DB, derived and growing daily). Restore was actually performed, not assumed. Bucket lifecycle is `daysFromHidingToDeleting: 1` — **without it a nightly 250MB DB version eats the 10GB free tier in six weeks, invisibly**, because the size check only counts current files. Setup and the two outstanding items (store the crypt password + salt OFF the Pi; regenerate the B2 master key) are in `backend/scripts/backup-offsite-SETUP.md`.

### Tests
- ⚠ **`npm test` must never touch the real vault (#119).** `node --test` globs `test-*.js`, so four Tier smoke scripts used to run on every invocation while asserting nothing — and `test-tier1.js` defaulted `OBSIDIAN_VAULT_PATH` to the REAL vault and created notes in it. They are now `backend/scripts/smoke-*.js` (the rename is what removes them from discovery), refuse to run without an explicit `OBSIDIAN_VAULT_PATH`, and `no-live-vault-in-tests.test.js` pins it. Keep it that way.
- **A test that pins a date must pin EVERY call it makes.** `outcomes.recent`/`trend` resolved "now" themselves while their siblings `computeWeek`/`snapshot` took an anchor, so a pinned fixture only half-pinned the module and the suite broke on a date rollover rather than a code change. Both now take an anchor, and a test asserts the anchor is HONOURED so a silent revert to `new Date()` fails in the week it is written.
- **A green suite says nothing about routing.** Call any new route against the running server before calling it done — a literal path registered after a sibling parameterised one is parsed as its parameter.
